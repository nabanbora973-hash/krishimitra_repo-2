Privacy Policy

KrishiMitra · last updated 1 November 2026

The short version: KrishiMitra works without an account. Your farm details, crop, season and sale records are stored on your phone. We do not sell data and we do not run advertising or tracking SDKs.

1. Who we are

KrishiMitra is an agricultural decision-support app for farmers in Assam, India. It shows daily mandi prices, live weather and a sell/hold recommendation. Contact: nabamborah@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

  • Your farm profile — village/town, district, area, crop and expected harvest.
  • Your season details and any sale records you enter.
  • The last price list the app successfully downloaded, so it still works offline.

This information stays in your browser/app storage on the device. Uninstalling the app, or using Clear data in Android settings, removes it.

3. What leaves your device

Market prices

Requests go to our own Cloudflare Worker, which forwards them to the Government of India open-data platform (data.gov.in / AGMARKNET). The request contains only a crop name and the state — no personal information.

Weather

Weather is fetched from Open-Meteo using the coordinates of your farm (or of the town you typed). No account or identifier is attached. See Open-Meteo terms.

If you choose to sign in

Signing in is optional. If you use a Google account or a phone number, authentication is handled by Google Firebase. We store only your account identifier and the profile you enter, so your data can follow you to a new phone. You can use the app entirely without this.

4. What we never do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not read your contacts, photos, SMS or call logs.
  • We do not share your farm details with traders or mandis.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Location (optional) — only when you tap “Use my location”, to measure distance to mandis and fetch weather for your field. Denying it changes nothing else: you can type your village instead.
  • Notifications (optional) — for price alerts you set yourself.
  • Internet — to fetch prices and weather.

6. Data honesty

Prices are labelled LIVE only when they come from the government feed. If the feed is unreachable the app shows LAST KNOWN (a saved earlier figure, with its date) or DEMO (an illustrative prototype dataset). It never presents an invented number as a live government price.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your choices

  • Use the app without signing in.
  • Deny location and type your village instead.
  • Delete everything with Android Settings → Apps → KrishiMitra → Storage → Clear data.
  • Ask us to delete a cloud account and its data by writing to the contact address above; we will do it within 30 days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears at this page with a new date. Material changes will be noted in the app.
